At a glance

HabitFlow has no account system or developer-operated habit-data server. Your habit details and check-ins are stored on your device. The app does not include an advertising or analytics SDK. If lifetime billing is configured in a release, RevenueCat and Google Play process purchase-related information to provide and verify the purchase.

Information processed by HabitFlow

HabitFlow does not ask you to create an account. When you use the app, it processes the information you choose to enter, including:

  • Habit names and optional descriptions, selected icons and colors, and chosen weekdays.
  • Habit creation or archive dates and the dates you mark a habit complete.
  • Optional reminder times and the local identifiers used to manage scheduled reminders.
  • Your reminder and light/dark appearance preferences.

HabitFlow's app code does not send this habit content to a developer-operated server. The project contains no app analytics or advertising SDK and does not request access to contacts or location. Purchase-related processing by third parties is described below.

If you email the support address, we receive the email address and message you choose to send, and use them to respond to your request.

Local storage

Habit records, completion history, and preferences are saved in app-specific storage on your Android device. HabitFlow does not provide account-based syncing or backup to a HabitFlow server. Android or device backup features may handle app data according to your device and Google account settings; those services are controlled by their providers.

Reminders and notifications

If you set a reminder and enable reminders, HabitFlow asks Android for notification permission. The app schedules reminders locally on your device for the weekdays and time you choose. A reminder notification can display the habit name you entered. HabitFlow does not use a remote push-notification server or send habit reminders through a developer-operated service.

You can turn reminders off in HabitFlow Settings or change notification permission in Android Settings. Notifications may be visible on your lock screen depending on your device settings.

Lifetime in-app purchases

HabitFlow offers a one-time lifetime purchase for premium access; it is not a subscription. The purchase feature works only in app builds where billing and the purchase product have been configured. In such a build, the RevenueCat SDK is used to retrieve the offer, complete or restore a purchase, and check whether lifetime access is active.

When you use this feature, RevenueCat and Google Play may process purchase and transaction details, app/device technical information, and identifiers needed to associate and verify the purchase. The app does not pass your habit names, descriptions, or completion history to the purchase SDK. Google Play handles the store checkout; HabitFlow does not receive your full payment-card details.

Purchase records and related information are handled under the providers' policies: RevenueCat Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

Third-party services and sharing

HabitFlow does not sell your personal information. The app's only optional third-party processing integration is billing: RevenueCat supports purchase status and entitlement checks, and Google Play provides the Android store checkout. These services process information under their own privacy policies, linked above. The Android operating system may also process app and notification data as part of providing the device platform.

No advertising, marketing analytics, or crash-reporting SDK is included in the app's declared dependencies. If you contact support, your message is handled by the email services involved in sending and receiving it.

Data retention and deletion

Your habit data remains in the app's local storage until you delete it, reset the app data, or remove the app. You can delete an individual habit in Settings, or use Settings > Reset all data to remove all habits, completion history, preferences, and HabitFlow-scheduled reminders from the device. Android device backups, if enabled, are managed separately by Android or your backup provider.

Google Play and RevenueCat may retain purchase records under their own policies and legal requirements. Resetting HabitFlow data does not cancel, refund, or erase a store purchase record. Support emails are retained as needed to handle the correspondence.

Security

HabitFlow stores habit information in app-specific device storage and relies on Android's standard app isolation. The app does not add a separate password or custom database-encryption layer. Protect your device with its available security settings. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children's privacy

HabitFlow is not directed to children under 13 and is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us by email, contact us so we can review and address the request.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when HabitFlow's features or data practices change. The latest version and its effective date will be posted on this page. Please review it from time to time.
